# Investment Theses

The pitch behind each holding — **exactly three thesis points per ticker**, surfaced
in the stock page's **Thesis** tab. Fill these in as the team writes them up.

## How it's used
- The Thesis tab shows the **most recent** dated block for a ticker. To update a thesis,
  add a new dated block above the old one (or just edit in place) — the newest date wins.
- Keep it to three numbered points; the tab renders them as three cards.

## Format (per holding)
```
### TICKER — Name

**2026-06-15 · A. Hull**
1. First thesis point.
2. Second thesis point.
3. Third thesis point.
```
`**YYYY-MM-DD · analyst**` header, then a numbered 1–3 list. Leave blank until written.
